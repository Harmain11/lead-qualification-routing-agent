Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026 · Privacy contact: harmainmughal8@gmail.com

Draft for testing: This notice describes the current app in general terms and is not legal advice. “Ingenuity Systems” is the operating brand shown in Corral; the formal legal entity and specific governing-law venue have not been verified. Have qualified counsel review it for the countries where you operate before commercial use.

1. Who this notice covers

Corral is a lead qualification, routing, analytics, and workspace service operated under the Ingenuity Systems brand. This notice covers information processed through the app, its account and workspace features, and integrations that an organization chooses to connect. The service is currently being tested with users in Pakistan and the United States.

2. Information processed

  • Account and workspace details: name, work email, password hash, Google account identifier when Google sign-in is used, workspace name, role, membership, invitation, and optional profile details such as job title, phone, biography, or avatar.
  • Lead and business information: lead name, email, phone, job title, company, industry, team size, revenue or budget ranges, location, requested service, message, source, qualification results, routing history, and CRM delivery status.
  • Connected-service data: when an administrator connects an account, Corral may process the lead records and mailbox messages needed for the selected import, notification, analysis, or synchronization feature. Connection status, account labels, and permission metadata are also stored.
  • Operational records: security and authentication events, timestamps, workspace actions, integration events, and error or request information needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

3. How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts; isolate workspaces; receive, qualify, route, and display leads; send alerts or configured confirmation messages; synchronize records with services an administrator connects; provide support; and protect, debug, and maintain Corral. We do not sell lead or account information or use it for advertising.

4. Connected providers

These providers receive information only when a user or workspace administrator chooses the related feature. Their own privacy terms also apply:

  • Google: sign-in receives basic identity information (verified email and profile name). If Gmail is separately connected, Corral can read the approved intake mailbox and send configured lead-confirmation email. If Google Calendar is separately connected, see “Google Calendar data” below.
  • Microsoft Outlook: if connected, Corral can read the approved inbox for configured lead intake.
  • Salesforce and HubSpot: if connected, Corral can import configured lead/contact data and perform user-authorized record updates.
  • Slack: if connected, Corral can send configured lead notifications to the selected workspace/channel.
  • OpenAI: if a workspace administrator configures an OpenAI key, selected lead information may be sent for context or intent analysis. OpenAI API usage is billed to that workspace’s OpenAI account, not as a Corral payment.
  • Hosting and infrastructure: infrastructure providers, including Replit when used to publish or run this workspace, may process information to host and secure the service.

Provider privacy information: Google, Microsoft, Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, OpenAI, and Replit.

Google Calendar data

When a workspace administrator connects Google Calendar, Corral requests permission to read primary-calendar metadata and free/busy availability, and to create calendar events. Corral uses this data only to show meeting availability and to create a meeting after it is selected through the scheduling workflow.

For availability checks, Corral uses the primary calendar’s identifier, display name and time zone, and the busy start and end times returned for the requested time range. It does not need to read existing event titles or descriptions to check availability. When a meeting is booked, Corral sends the selected meeting’s title, description, date and time, and the invitee’s name and email address to Google Calendar so Google can create the event and send its invitation.

Calendar data is not sold or used for advertising or to train generalized AI models. It is shared with Google as needed to provide the calendar features you choose and is available to workspace members according to their permissions. Corral retains connected-account credentials while the connection is active and removes its stored credentials when you disconnect it. Disconnecting does not delete events already created in Google Calendar; you can manage those events in Google Calendar and revoke Corral’s access in your Google Account settings. Corral’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. Cookies and browser storage

Corral uses authentication and CSRF cookies to keep sign-in sessions working and to protect requests. It also uses browser local storage for display preferences such as theme and selected Dashboard or Analytics cards. The current app does not include advertising trackers.

6. Sharing, storage, and retention

Workspace information is visible to members according to their assigned permissions. We share information with connected providers only to carry out the features the workspace has enabled, or when needed for security, legal compliance, or service operation. Provider systems may process data in countries other than Pakistan or the United States.

We retain information while it is needed to operate the account and workspace, and longer when needed for security, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. Workspace administrators can manage or delete lead records. Some copies may remain in protected backups for a limited period. Contact us for account or privacy requests; requests about organization-managed lead data may need to be handled by that organization’s administrator.

7. Security

Corral uses account authentication, workspace access permissions, server-side membership checks, and protected session handling. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should use strong, unique credentials and connect only accounts they are authorized to use.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

You can update available profile details, manage connected providers, and ask your workspace administrator about lead records. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of personal information. Contact us below and identify the account or workspace involved; we may need to verify your request and coordinate with the organization that supplied the data.

9. Children

Corral accounts are for people aged 13 or older. The service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek their information. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us so it can be reviewed and removed as appropriate.

10. Testing and future changes

Corral is currently free for testing and does not collect payment-card or subscription billing details through the app. If paid features are introduced, we will update this notice before those features are offered. We may update this page as the service changes; the date above indicates the latest revision.
