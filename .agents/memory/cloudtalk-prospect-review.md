---
name: CloudTalk prospect review
description: Product rule for reviewing CloudTalk call history and creating leads.
---

Show recent CloudTalk calls even when they do not match an existing lead. Use call transcripts and summaries to flag likely prospects, but never create a lead automatically; require a user to review details and explicitly choose Add lead.

**Why:** The user chose recommendation-only because calls may be support conversations, wrong numbers, or otherwise not prospects.

**How to apply:** Keep global call history independent of lead selection, show the assessment as a recommendation, and use a confirmed manual lead-creation flow.
