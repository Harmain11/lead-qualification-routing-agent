Prioritize preventing unrelated emails from becoming leads, rather than merely increasing qualification scores. Routine promotional or marketing messages sent to the workspace inbox are not leads, even when they contain business language. Generic business words alone are not enough evidence of a buying inquiry. Keep genuine inquiries about workspace services and replies in existing sales conversations eligible.

**Why:** The user clarified that emails were extracting irrelevant things alongside leads and chose to exclude routine marketing email sent to the workspace from lead qualification.

**How to apply:** Evaluate the sender's current, authored inquiry separately from quoted history, automated messages, promotional marketing, newsletters, and unrelated contact activity. Exclude unrelated messages before lead creation. Preserve genuine sales inquiries and thread history.
