Prioritize preventing unrelated emails from becoming leads, rather than merely increasing qualification scores. Generic business words alone are not enough evidence of a buying inquiry. Keep replies in existing sales conversations as interactions rather than new leads.

**Why:** The user clarified that emails were extracting irrelevant things alongside leads.

**How to apply:** Evaluate the sender's current, authored inquiry separately from quoted history, automated messages, newsletters, and unrelated contact activity. Preserve genuine sales inquiries and thread history.
