---
name: Meeting scheduling safety
description: Product requirements for Gmail-based meeting recommendations in Corral.
---

Read the tracked Gmail conversation for recent meeting requests, email numbered available times to the lead, and book only after the lead clearly confirms one numbered option. Do not book ambiguous replies. Keep workspace boundaries and the existing manual booking path.

**Why:** The user chose “Email available times, then book the lead’s confirmed choice” instead of the earlier admin-confirmation flow.

**How to apply:** Send options in the tracked Gmail thread, accept only a clear selection, then add the attendee to the connected calendar. Keep ambiguous replies and requests for different times visible for team follow-up.
