---
name: Lead assistant action safety
description: User-selected confirmation boundaries for actions proposed by Corral on lead pages.
---

Corral may answer questions, add details, and propose actions across the lead page. Require explicit user confirmation before sending emails, booking meetings, syncing a CRM record, or deleting a lead. Proposed actions must not be described as completed until the corresponding action succeeds.

**Why:** The user chose broad assistant access on lead pages and specifically required confirmation for emails, bookings, CRM syncs, and deletes.

**How to apply:** Preserve existing permission checks and action-specific confirmation steps whenever adding or changing lead-assistant actions.
