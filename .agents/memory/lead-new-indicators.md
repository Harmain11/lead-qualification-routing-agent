---
name: New lead indicators
description: Lead numbers stay out of the UI; unseen new leads use per-lead and tab dots.
---

Do not display sequential lead numbers. Show a dot beside each new, unseen lead and a navigation dot for the Leads area; Manual Review also gets a dot when it contains unseen new leads. Clear a lead's dot when that lead is opened, and clear each tab dot only when all relevant new leads have been opened.

**Why:** The user chose both per-lead and tab indicators instead of lead numbers, with the tab indicator remaining until all new leads are opened.

**How to apply:** Keep numeric identifiers internal. Preserve the dot behavior across visits and scope it to the active user and workspace.
