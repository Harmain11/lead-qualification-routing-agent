---
name: Gmail AI lead sync
description: Product behavior for Gmail lead detection and safe handling of AI failures.
---

Keep non-deterministic AI classification as the primary Gmail lead detector and favor recall for plausible early-stage business inquiries; do not add a rigid keyword gate after an AI-positive result. Analyze the whole thread as participants and candidate prospects, assign roles, and allow multiple distinct leads per thread. Treat configured internal domains and known workspace members as internal, but retain external referrers as context. Unwrap internal notification emails such as “Ingenuity Lead Notifications”: never use the wrapper sender, label, or mailbox owner as the lead; extract externally evidenced people from embedded fields and retain the wrapper as provenance. Do not default to the mailbox sender, latest sender, or To recipient. Referred candidates without email are allowed only with a supported name plus reliable phone/company/referral evidence; uncertain identity requires manual review. Preserve candidate-specific evidence, role, referral relationship, thread/message provenance, and stable candidate-level idempotency. Do not create leads for people appearing only in CC, signatures, or unrelated mentions. Verify AI evidence as contiguous source excerpts after normalizing Unicode punctuation and whitespace, but do not accept paraphrases. Missing or invalid AI credentials must be surfaced as a configuration problem; transient per-message AI failure must skip and audit that message while allowing the rest of the sync to continue. Never log email bodies or credentials.

**Why:** The user requires Gmail referrals to remain separate from referrers and internal delivery wrappers to be unwrapped so the embedded external lead—not a teammate or notification label—is imported. Strict byte-for-byte evidence matching can also reject valid evidence with harmless formatting differences.

**How to apply:** When changing Gmail acquisition, classification, background processing, or status reporting, preserve AI classification, high recall without a regex veto, whole-thread context, internal-domain/member filtering, candidate role/referral evidence, phone-only support with review for uncertainty, candidate-level idempotency, safe per-message audit reasons, bounded retries, and clear progress/error states.
