---
name: Separate Corral pipelines
description: Product rule for keeping lead qualification and sales progression distinct.
---

Corral's Pipeline should retain its qualification progression and add sales progression as a separate view rather than replacing or conflating the two.

**Why:** The user asked for a sales pipeline alongside the existing Pipeline.

**How to apply:** When changing Corral's pipeline UI or analytics, preserve the qualification flow and track sales progression separately using recorded outreach, meeting, and outcome data.
