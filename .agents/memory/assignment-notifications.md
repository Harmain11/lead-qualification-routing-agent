---
name: Lead assignment notifications
description: Product rule for notifying workspace members when a lead is assigned to them.
---

Notify the assigned workspace member inside Corral whenever a lead becomes assigned to them, whether through automatic routing or manual assignment. Reassigning the same lead to the same member is not a new assignment; unassigning does not send a notification.

**Why:** the user asked that assigned members be notified in the platform so they know a lead was assigned to them.

**How to apply:** Keep notifications durable, scoped to the recipient and workspace, visible in the in-app inbox until read, and separate from workspace-wide new-lead indicators and external Slack notifications.
