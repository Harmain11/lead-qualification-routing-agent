---
name: Settings category organization
description: Corral settings should separate company-wide controls from personal and team account settings.
---

Organize Settings into two categories: Company for workspace-wide configuration and User for personal account and team access. Keep existing options, authorization checks, save behavior, and direct destinations reachable within the relevant category. Settings cards should be collapsible and default to a compact state; expanded cards should fit their content instead of stretching to fill the page.

**Why:** The user asked to separate Company and User settings and make the cards collapsible to reduce clutter and empty space.

**How to apply:** When changing Settings navigation or adding controls, place workspace-level settings under Company and account/member settings under User; preserve capabilities while using collapsible cards and content-sized layouts to reduce clutter.
